# What are the economic imperatives of this platform? 

The first and most important economic imperative is that it must be a zero cost solution for the reviewers, people who are providing this review service, which we call - Proof of Attention (PoA) . In this first iteration, as a reference architecture, we have implemented three  free Tools ( Free as in free beers) . 

## The first is the Research Ingestion service which leverages Github login and Github issue management as a public free infrastructure. No, we know that Github itself is not open platform. It is owned by Microsoft. But in AI era it's ubiquity is given. It has become a primitive service for the developers and researchers alike. Git itself is a open solution. The Github is a platform. But you know, we don't want to be fussy. 

## The second aspect is the publication. We are using MD book which is a Rust based free and open utility for publishing. In addition to mdbook, we have built utilities around it. For example, we have MD ingest service which can take any raw input in a Python script format and publish it on mdBook without the reviewer having to understand the intricacies of mdbook. The second is we have again chosen Github pages to publish the research free of charge,  and finally it is enabled as Progressive Web App so that people can download and read it offline without having to pay the app store taxes. 

One of the key aspect of this publication is the workflow, wherein we have structured it into three main categories. The "mempool" where we intake the new research, the template where we host 21 research papers or as we call them  "episodes" for public scrutiny, and then finally a block of 21 Episodes is added to the chain. We highly recommend this structure to be maintained by any open reviewer to kind of provide a unified experience to people submitting research as well as people referring to the research or reading the research as a narrative. The structure is obviously inspired by Bitcoin blockchain, but it doesn't have any mechanism of proof of work. Instead, what we want to build in is proof of attention.   

And final piller of publication is -  the blocks of chain are added to a publicly shared notebookLM so that  anyone could query the research without having to go through episodic reading if they wish.

# Persona of a reviewer and proof of attention.

A reviewer is an expert in a specific field in which they are willing to contribute their attention for reviewing the research of fellow practitioners. But in addition they also must have bare minimum technical knowledge to be able to to host this platform, improve it and tailor it to the requirement of their own skills as well as their field of research. For example, since for us, writing mathematical equations in publication was important. We developed a tool based on mdbook for rendering mathematical symbols using Katex. Now if you are comfortable in Latex, you can do that too. So there is no hard and fast criteria about how you are going to use tools. It all depends upon your area of expertise as a reviewer and your field. But the most important thing is as a reviewer you are providing attention. You may conduct research of your own and put it on the platform, but the primary goal is while you are listening to the audio files again and again, while you are clipping short infographics, while you are posting it on on social media platforms and while you are responding to the comments of the people, you should be able to -  Provide your full attention and the success of your Your media dissemination is proof of your attention. 

There is no monetary benefit in becoming a reviewer, so obviously you should be financially independent. But one of the great benefit is that you can gain popularity on social media and with your fellow researchers. It's the work of public good, and public good always gets honored with love and affection of people. At least that's what we hope.
