SMS Architecture

The Three Pillars of Shutri Media Solution

A comprehensive, end-to-end ecosystem engineered for research intake, publishing, and multi-modal promotion.

Pillar 01

Research Intake (`shutri`)

Submissions enter via open GitHub Issue Management. Research undergoes initial deepMind AI (`dm`) evaluation, generating an open synthesis report in the Mempool for peer review collaboration.

  • ▪ Open GitHub Issue Substrate
  • ▪ Transparent AI Perspective (`dm`)
  • ▪ CC BY-SA 4.0 Open License
Pillar 02

Research Publishing (`mdIngest` & `deepDive`)

Powered by the open-source Rust engine mdIngest (`coolchain`) to sanitize KaTeX math and citations into deepDive ([deepdive.shutri.com](https://deepdive.shutri.com))—a high-performance PWA ledger governed by the Law of 21.

  • ▪ Lossless Python Payload (`extract.py`)
  • ▪ Absolute KaTeX Math Hardening
  • ▪ Progressive Web App (PWA) Precache
Pillar 03

Research Promotion (`ddma`)

Driven by DDMA (DeepDive Media Automator). Uses word-level Whisper transcription and parameter-tuned `acrossfade` stings to output master podcasts and 740x740 Square Videos (<20 MB for Nostr relay compliance).

  • ▪ 740x740 Square Video Standard
  • ▪ Nostr Protocol Compliance (< 20MB)
  • ▪ Mosaic AI Motion Graphics
Lifecycle Protocol

The Journey of Knowledge

How a research draft moves from an initial submission into an immutable ledger block.

1

1. Submitter Draft (Staging)

Author submits research URL & thesis via native form. Ingestion is 100% free.

2

2. Mempool (AI Ingestion & Review)

Gemini & `dm` evaluate core logic, generate an initial perspective report, and publish to the Mempool.

3

3. Template (Human-AI Production)

Author & reviewer build multi-modal assets: NotebookLM podcast audio, DDMA 740x740 videos, and Mosaic infographics.

4

4. Chain (Vector DB Consensus)

Once 21 verified papers pass public review, the block is minted to the permanent deepMind Vector DB chain.
