Reference Architecture (v0.1)

The Shutri Manifesto

As the cost of generating research approaches zero, the limiting factor becomes expert review. The Shutri Manifesto defines a practical reference architecture for conducting open, AI-assisted research review using freely available tools.

Empowering Domain Experts to Launch Independent Peer Review Services

Shutri is built for domain experts, independent researchers, and specialized communities who want to start peer review services in their own domain of expertise. We operate deepDive as an open reference ledger and authors are welcome to submit research, but the true goal is to democratize the peer review process itself—enabling anyone to operate an open, AI-assisted review node in their own discipline.

⚡ Zero Operational Cost

Minimizes recurring infrastructure costs so that participation is limited only by reviewer attention, not financial resources.

🔓 Public by Default

Research, discussions, revisions, and published ledgers remain publicly accessible without paywalls.

🧩 Modular & Extensible

Independent capabilities (mdIngest, DDMA, mdBook) can be replaced or adapted to any discipline's workflow.

🤝 AI-Assisted, Human-Reviewed

AI accelerates discovery & synthesis while human reviewers remain strictly responsible for every published conclusion.

🧠 Proof of Attention

Reviewer contributions generate visible public artifacts (publications, podcasts, diagrams) constituting a verifiable, open record of careful intellectual work.

Lifecycle Protocol

The Journey of Knowledge

How a research draft moves from an initial submission into an immutable ledger block.

1

1. Submitter Draft (Staging)

Author submits research URL & thesis via native form. Ingestion is 100% free.

2

2. Mempool (AI Ingestion & Review)

Gemini & `dm` evaluate core logic, generate an initial perspective report, and publish to the Mempool.

3

3. Template (Human-AI Production)

Author & reviewer build multi-modal assets: NotebookLM podcast audio, DDMA 740x740 videos, and Mosaic infographics.

4

4. Chain (Vector DB Consensus)

Once 21 verified papers pass public review, the block is minted to the permanent deepMind Vector DB chain.

Etymology & Sacred Geometry

What is in the Name? — Shutri (शु-त्रि)

शु Shu = Auspicious & Pious

Derived from Sanskrit Shubh / Shu, representing virtuous intent, pure knowledge, and sacred open dialogue free from insular paywalls.

त्रि Tri = The Three Pillars

Represents the SMS operational trinity: Pillar 1 Intake (`shutri`), Pillar 2 Publishing (`mdIngest`/`deepDive`), and Pillar 3 Promotion (`ddma`).

श्रुति Śruti = "That Which is Heard"

Synthesizes direct oral knowledge transmission (Śruti) with structured algorithmic logic (Sūtra): Intake hears the raw inquiry → Publishing threads the immutable ledger → Promotion broadcasts audio podcasts & 740x740 square videos back into public sound space.
